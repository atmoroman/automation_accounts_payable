# Policy PM-01: Invoice Approval Limits

**Owner:** Finance / Accounts Payable · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose
Defines who may approve supplier invoices and up to which amount, to prevent unauthorised spending.

## 2. Approval thresholds (net amount, EUR equivalent)
| Net amount | Required approver |
|---|---|
| up to 1,000 EUR | Cost-centre owner |
| 1,000.01 – 10,000 EUR | Department head |
| 10,000.01 – 50,000 EUR | Department head **and** Finance controller |
| above 50,000 EUR | CFO |

## 3. Rules
- Amounts in other currencies are converted using the ECB reference rate on the invoice date.
- Splitting one purchase into several smaller invoices to stay below a threshold is prohibited and must be reported to Internal Audit.
- An approver may not approve an invoice for a purchase they requested themselves (four-eyes principle).

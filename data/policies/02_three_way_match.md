# Policy PM-02: Three-Way Match

**Owner:** Accounts Payable · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Rule
An invoice may only be paid when it matches **(1)** an approved purchase order (PO) and **(2)** a goods receipt or service entry sheet.

## 2. Tolerances
- Price tolerance: invoice unit price may exceed the PO price by at most **2%** or **50 EUR**, whichever is lower.
- Quantity tolerance: invoiced quantity may not exceed received quantity.
- Invoices outside tolerance are blocked for payment and routed to the buyer for clarification.

## 3. Exceptions (no PO required)
- Utilities, telecom and rent with a valid framework contract.
- Invoices below 250 EUR net from pre-approved catalogue suppliers.
- All other non-PO invoices require Department head approval and a written justification.

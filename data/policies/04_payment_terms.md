# Policy PM-04: Payment Terms

**Owner:** Treasury · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

- Standard payment term: **30 days** from invoice receipt.
- Small and medium suppliers (SME flag in master data): **14 days**.
- Early-payment discounts (e.g. 2% within 10 days) should be used whenever the discount exceeds the company's short-term cost of capital.
- Payment terms shorter than 7 days require Treasury approval and are a red flag for urgency-based fraud.
- Payment runs take place every Tuesday and Thursday.

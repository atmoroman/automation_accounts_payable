# Policy PM-03: Supplier Onboarding and Master Data

**Owner:** Procurement · **Effective:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Approved suppliers
Invoices may only be paid to suppliers that exist in the supplier master data with status **Active**.

## 2. Onboarding requirements
- Valid VAT identification number, verified via the EU VIES service for EU suppliers.
- Bank details confirmed by a call-back to a phone number from an independent source.
- Signed Supplier Code of Conduct (incl. anti-bribery and human-rights clauses).

## 3. Bank detail changes
A request to change a supplier's bank account is a common fraud pattern. Changes must be verified by call-back and approved by two people from Procurement. Payment to new bank details is held for **5 business days**.

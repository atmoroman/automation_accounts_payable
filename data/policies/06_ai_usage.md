# Policy PM-06: Use of AI in Finance Processes

**Owner:** Finance & Compliance · **Effective:** 1 June 2026

- AI systems may **propose** invoice data, account assignments and anomaly flags, but a human must **approve** every payment.
- Personal data (names of private individuals, IBANs of individuals) must be masked before sending documents to external AI services.
- Every AI suggestion must be logged with the model name, input reference and timestamp, so decisions can be audited.
- Users must be able to see *why* the system flagged an invoice (explainability).
- AI models are re-evaluated every quarter against a labelled test set; accuracy below the agreed threshold triggers a review.

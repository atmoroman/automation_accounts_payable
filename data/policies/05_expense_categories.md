# Policy PM-05: Spend Categories and Restrictions

**Owner:** Procurement · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## IT hardware
Laptops and monitors must be ordered from the IT catalogue. Laptops above 1,500 EUR per unit need IT Security sign-off.

## External consulting
Consulting requires a statement of work (SOW). Day rates above 1,500 EUR need Department head approval. Travel expenses are reimbursed at cost and must be itemised.

## Catering and events
Catering for internal meetings is limited to **25 EUR per person**. Alcohol may not be purchased on company funds except for approved client events.

## Marketing materials
Print orders above 5,000 EUR require three competing quotes.
